About

I design structures and see them through to construction. Over 15 years I’ve worked on hospitals, metro stations, power plants, reservoirs and homes, at firms like Skidmore, Owings & Merrill and Parsons Brinckerhoff, across the United States and Singapore.

Today I run Aphora Group, a residential design and construction management company in the Bay Area. I take projects from drawings and permits all the way to final inspection.

Structural design

Steel, concrete, wood and masonry, for gravity, seismic, wind and vibration.

Permits and plan check

Complete permit drawing sets and coordination with Bay Area planning and building departments.

Construction management

Site inspection, subcontractors, schedules and budgets, from groundbreaking to handover.
