About

Over 20 years I’ve worked on hospitals, metro stations, power plants, reservoirs and homes, at firms like Skidmore, Owings & Merrill and Parsons Brinckerhoff, across the United States and Singapore.

Today I lead Plankore, a San Francisco team of structural engineers and AI Ph.D.s. We prepare takeoffs and permit drawings for builders, and we’re building the AI takeoff and permit software I always wanted to use. I’ve also worked as a plan checker, so I know the permit process from every side.

Structural design

Steel, concrete, wood and masonry, for gravity, seismic, wind and vibration.

Permits and plan check

Complete permit drawing sets and coordination with Bay Area planning and building departments.

Construction management

Site inspection, subcontractors, schedules and budgets, from groundbreaking to handover.
