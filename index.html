About

I’ve sat in every seat on a project: the engineer who designs it, the builder who puts it up, and the city reviewer who approves it.

I designed hospitals, metro stations and Singapore’s largest prestressed concrete water tank at firms like Skidmore, Owings & Merrill and Parsons Brinckerhoff. In Singapore I worked as a resident engineer on high-rise sites, inspecting every pile, beam and slab as it went in. Back in California I ran my own design-build firm, then spent two years checking permit plans for the City of Fremont.

Now I co-lead Plankore, a San Francisco team of structural engineers and AI Ph.D.s. We prepare permit drawings and construction takeoffs for builders, and we’re building the AI software I always wished I had.
